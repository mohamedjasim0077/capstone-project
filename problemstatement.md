Problem Statement

Traditional clothing rental services often depend on physical stores, manual booking, limited clothing choices, and time-consuming communication between customers and clothing owners. Customers may find it difficult to discover suitable outfits, check availability, compare rental prices, and manage bookings conveniently. Clothing owners also face challenges in listing their products, tracking rentals, managing availability, and receiving payments securely. The proposed Clothing Rental Marketplace Platform provides a digital solution that connects customers with clothing owners through a single online marketplace. Owners can register and upload clothing items with details such as category, size, rental price, availability, images, and descriptions. Customers can search and filter available clothes, view item details, select rental dates, place bookings, and make payments through the platform.
